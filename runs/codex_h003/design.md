# H003 activation localization and development design

Frozen on November 26, 2026, before any H003 forward pass or weight edit. The starting revision is `cb8d828a125fe7f409bd3bdcf5409fba6e8879ec`. The experiment uses the pinned local donor and recipient in the H003 handoff, with the two development JSONLs as its only problem sources.

## Hypotheses

1. On carry/borrow expressions and numbers embedded in GSM8K prose, a subset of the donor's DeltaNet heads will read numeric tokens more strongly than their matched recipient heads. The middle-to-late blocks (especially layers 12–18, zero-based) are plausible sites for maintaining operand information. Inspect linear-attention layers 8–22, where both models have 16 heads of width 128. Match routing by observed behavior because a head index need not denote the same function at both widths.
2. Zeroing the selected head's contribution before its output projection will reduce the donor's correct-first-digit margin more than zeroing the matched recipient head. This measures an intervention on a forced numeric readout. It does not establish that the donor solved the problem or that the head's decay parameters account for its causal effect.
3. Transplanting the donor's decay parameters at this head, adjusted for the recipient's digit-conditioned input level, may improve complete carry solutions while retaining GSM8K performance. The effect could be small or negative. The edit changes at most two stored scalars.

## Frozen procedure

- Use the first 16 carry and first 16 GSM8K rows in file order for calibration. Append the fixed assistant cue `The final answer is \boxed{` to the standard no-thinking chat prompt. The answer itself never enters a model. Score the first digit of the absolute gold answer against the strongest of the nine other digit logits. Use original question token offsets to identify decimal digits and other non-whitespace tokens. Do not treat the template or answer cue as question tokens.
- For each candidate layer, match heads one-to-one using the Hungarian assignment on the mean of two Pearson correlations over paired question tokens. The features are log decay magnitude `A_log + log(softplus(a + dt_bias))` and write gate `sigmoid(b)`. For each head compute the log ratio of mean post-gate head RMS on digits to that on other tokens. Rank matched pairs by donor-minus-recipient log-ratio on prompts where the donor digit margin exceeds the recipient's (all prompts of a family if none qualify). Give each family equal weight and multiply by the nonnegative part of its routing correlation. Take the best pair from each of the best two distinct layers. Break ranking ties by lower layer then lower recipient head.
- On the first six calibration prompts of each family, ablate each shortlisted head at every position, once in the donor and once in the recipient. Rank by the mean donor minus recipient drop in digit margin, equally weighting families. Select one pair, retaining the signed effects even if both disappoint. Clean calls plus ablations use 56 singleton forwards per model. Record every donor call through the T2 toolbox and enforce the 64-call ceiling across restarts.
- For the selected head, take the per-model median across 32 per-prompt means of the decay projection `a` on digit tokens. Target the donor's `A_log` and `dt_bias + median_d - median_r` in the recipient. Interpolate from its pristine values at strengths 0.5 and 1.0, rounding once to the stored dtype. Freeze the localization and offsets for replay and C5. A C5 replacement supplies both complete donor vectors and goes through the same index, offset, interpolation and rounding.
- Generate ordinary uncued, greedy, no-thinking solutions on MPS in bfloat16, at most 1,024 new tokens, using the project parser. Screen both strengths on rows 16–35 (zero-based, inclusive) of each family. Rank by total correct on these equally sized families, then by carry correct, then smaller strength. Return the selected nonzero edit even if both scores fall below C0, describing any regression. Score it once on rows 36–99 of each family. Independently score pristine C0 and the archived activation-assisted C4 on both slices. The 64+64 disjoint follow-up is development evidence. C5 behavioral scoring and blinded validation belong to independent verification.
- Direct model generation, checkpoint creation and checks run on the local host. H002's completed token counter implies $6.041536 shadow usage, leaving $5.958464 at the start of H003. The handoff quoted an earlier H002 snapshot. No paid services or checkpoint downloads are required.
